Behind the benchmark

I was curious about how good AI models have gotten at forecasting electricity prices. So I started comparing their forecasts and checking what a battery could earn by following them.

Hi! I'm Parth

I’m a DPhil researcher at the University of Oxford and a Principal Analyst at S&P Global, where I cover European power markets.
